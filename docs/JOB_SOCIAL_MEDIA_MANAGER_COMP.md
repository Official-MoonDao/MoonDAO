# Compensation recommendation — Social Media Manager (X + Instagram)

**Internal.** Not for the public posting. The posting states only the range and that it is
confirmed in the next Executive Branch budgeting cycle.

---

## Recommendation

**Publish $3,000–$4,500 per month** for ≤10 hours/week, with fiat and/or $MOONEY at the
contributor's election.

That is roughly **$70–$105/hour**, or **$36k–$54k/year** for the part-time commitment.

| Candidate profile | Monthly | Effective hourly |
|---|---|---|
| Meets most criteria — some space *or* crypto exposure, clean growth numbers, reliable execution | $2,000–$3,000 | $50–$75 |
| **Target hire** — documented results on relevant accounts, real lunar/space literacy, crypto and DAO fluency, a personal following that amplifies | **$3,000–$4,500** | **$70–$105** |
| Top-tier specialist with meaningful existing reach in the right circles and a track record on technical or decentralized projects | $4,500–$6,000+ | $100–$150 |

Anything under ~$2,000/month will not clear the bar the posting sets; it attracts generalists.
Above ~$5,000–$6,000/month we are competing with fuller-scope consulting engagements and should
be getting outsized leverage — a large relevant audience or an exceptional documented growth
history — in return.

## Why the higher end of part-time ranges

Ten hours a week is about 25% of a full-time load, so straight pro-rata of a mid-level full-time
social media salary ($70k–$90k base) would land near $1,500–$1,900/month. Three factors push
past that:

1. **Dual-niche premium.** Credible lunar/commercial-space literacy *and* crypto/DAO mechanics in
   the same person is rare. Niche specialization routinely adds 30–50% to rates, and this is two
   niches.
2. **We are selecting for a specialist, not an executor.** The posting demands documented prior
   growth and an existing audience. That is closer to a consultant profile than a coordinator,
   and it prices accordingly.
3. **Outcome accountability on a competitive channel.** X growth in space and crypto circles is
   hard-won, and success is measured in growth and audience quality rather than post volume.

Sanity check in the other direction: $3,000–$4,500/month at 10 hrs/week implies a full-time
equivalent in the $140k–$200k range. That is consistent with senior specialized rates once both
niche premiums are applied, and it is a range we should be comfortable defending publicly.

## Reference points

- Full-time remote US social media manager: ~$70k–$90k base mid-level, $90k–$140k+
  senior/specialist.
- Freelance intermediate (2–5 yrs, multi-platform, content + engagement): $40–$75/hr, or
  $1,500–$4,000/month.
- Freelance experienced/specialized (5+ yrs, strategy, proven growth, niche expertise):
  $75–$150+/hr, or $4,000–$8,000+/month for fuller scopes.
- Web3/crypto social and community roles carry a clear premium; full-time commonly $60k–$120k,
  freelance web3 social frequently $1,000–$3,000+/month.
- Space/aerospace social and community roles for technical audiences: ~$90k–$115k full-time.

## Structure

**Publish the range.** Three reasons: specialists at this level screen out unpriced postings, an
undisclosed range sits badly next to a fully public treasury, and a stated range does most of the
salary negotiation before the first call.

**Fiat/$MOONEY split, contributor's choice.** A lower cash component is common in DAO roles when
the token allocation has credible upside and the contributor is mission-aligned — but the
cash-equivalent should still land in the bands above. Do not use $MOONEY to discount the
engagement below market; use it to let an aligned contributor take upside.

**Monthly retainer, not hourly billing.** Hourly is fine for the trial. Ongoing, a fixed monthly
retainer against clear deliverables and a monthly report avoids scope creep in both directions
and avoids arguing about timesheets for a role judged on outcomes.

**Paid trial.** One to two weeks at the pro-rata rate for a real thread, a real carousel, and an
engagement plan. Roughly $750–$1,100 of exposure to de-risk a multi-month engagement, and it
means we never ask for spec work.

**Review cadence.** 90-day review against the targets agreed in week one, then quarterly. State
up front that strong results lead to a rate increase or expanded scope — a specialist reads a
static rate as a ceiling.

## Budget-cycle sequencing

The interview window is 6 weeks and the salary lands in the next Executive Branch budgeting
cycle. Two consequences worth planning for:

1. **Budget the top of the range, not the midpoint.** If the request goes in at $3,000 and the
   strongest candidate needs $4,500, we either lose them or re-open the request a cycle later. A
   $4,500/month line with the option to hire below it costs nothing if unused.
2. **Fund the trial out of the current cycle.** ~$1,000 for a paid trial should not wait on the
   next budgeting cycle; if it does, the 6-week window becomes 10 and the best candidates take
   other offers in the meantime.
