# FIRST TRACKS

**Owner:** MoonDAO

Competitor listings are editorial and based solely on public sources. No listed
organization has been contacted, has consented, or is affiliated with this prize.

## Resolution criteria

Which commercial rover leaves its lander and drives at least 10 metres first.
The rover is commercial and uncrewed. This is not a crewed rover race.

Evidence is public. The same settlement path as Touchdown applies: public
evidence, then a Senate vote, then a Safe transaction. See
[DEPRIZE_TOUCHDOWN.md](DEPRIZE_TOUCHDOWN.md).

The purse is a community payload purchase. See
[DEPRIZE_PAYLOAD_PURSE.md](DEPRIZE_PAYLOAD_PURSE.md).
