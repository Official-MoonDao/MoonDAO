# DePrize marketing checklist

**Owner:** MoonDAO communications  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-11

Use this list for every public DePrize announcement, thread, video, landing
page, or paid placement. It is an operations checklist, not legal advice.

## Required legend

Every announcement must carry this sentence, unaltered:

> Not available to U.S. persons or anyone located in the United States or another restricted jurisdiction.

The same sentence ships in DePrize page metadata, page footers, and the DePrize
legal documents. Do not paraphrase it in a way that narrows "U.S. persons" or
omits restricted jurisdictions.

## Paid targeting

- Do not buy ads, boosts, or sponsored placements targeted at the United States
  or at other restricted jurisdictions on the published Schedule A.
- Do not use lookalike, retargeting, or interest audiences that are built from
  U.S. users to promote DePrize.
- If a platform cannot exclude those locations, do not run the paid placement.

## What not to publish

- Do not tell employees, contractors, or the community how to bypass the
  country gate, VPN check, attestations, or permit.
- Do not share `DEPRIZE_ELIGIBILITY_BYPASS`, cron secrets, webhook URLs, or
  other environment values.
- Do not describe a VPN, relay, or "just connect from elsewhere" as a way to
  place a bet.
- Do not imply that DePrize is open to U.S. persons, U.S. entities, or people
  located in the United States.

## Before you hit publish

- [ ] The required legend is visible in the post, video description, or page
- [ ] Paid targeting excludes the United States and other restricted jurisdictions
- [ ] The draft does not include bypass instructions
- [ ] Links go to `/deprize` or the published legal documents, not to internal runbooks
