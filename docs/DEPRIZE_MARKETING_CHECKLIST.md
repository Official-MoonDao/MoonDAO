# DePrize marketing checklist

**Owner:** MoonDAO communications  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

Use this list for every public DePrize announcement, thread, video, landing
page, or paid placement. It is an operations checklist, not legal advice.

## Availability framing

DePrize predictions — including free picks and Citizen predictions weighted by
voting power — are open to anyone worldwide. Only **adding ETH to a prediction**
(buying Outcome Tokens in the ETH market) is restricted by jurisdiction.

Where DePrize pages, metadata, legal documents, or ETH-betting copy state the
availability rule, use this sentence:

> Predictions are open worldwide. Adding ETH to a prediction is unavailable in restricted jurisdictions.

Do not describe DePrize as a whole as "not available to U.S. persons" — free
predictions and voting-power forecasts are open globally.

## Paid targeting

- Do not buy ads, boosts, or sponsored placements that promote **adding ETH /
  ETH betting** targeted at the United States or at other restricted
  jurisdictions on the published Schedule A.
- Do not use lookalike, retargeting, or interest audiences built from restricted
  jurisdictions to promote ETH betting.
- If a platform cannot exclude those locations, do not run a paid ETH-betting
  placement.

## What not to publish

- Do not tell employees, contractors, or the community how to bypass the
  country gate, VPN check, attestations, or permit for adding ETH.
- Do not share `DEPRIZE_ELIGIBILITY_BYPASS`, cron secrets, webhook URLs, or
  other environment values.
- Do not describe a VPN, relay, or "just connect from elsewhere" as a way to
  add ETH to a prediction.
- Do not imply that adding ETH to a prediction is open in the United States or
  another restricted jurisdiction.

## Before you hit publish

- [ ] Copy distinguishes open worldwide predictions/voting-power forecasts from restricted ETH betting (where ETH betting is mentioned)
- [ ] Paid targeting for ETH betting excludes the United States and other restricted jurisdictions
- [ ] The draft does not include bypass instructions
- [ ] Links go to `/deprize`, `/updates/...`, or the published legal documents, not to internal runbooks
