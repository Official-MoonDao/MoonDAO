vMOONEY ([vote escrow](/docs/About/Glossary/Vote-Escrow) MOONEY) is a measure of the voting power of locked MOONEY. vMOONEY is granted in proportion to the amount and duration of the MOONEY locked. It is not a token that can be purchased on any marketplace.
