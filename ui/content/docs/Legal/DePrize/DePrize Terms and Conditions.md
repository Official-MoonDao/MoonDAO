## 4. What DePrize Is

**4.1 A non-custodial, on-chain prediction market.** For each DePrize, you may buy and sell Outcome Tokens representing the competing outcomes (the listed Competitors and, where applicable, the Open Field). Prices are set by an automated market maker and reflect the aggregate positions of participants; **prices are not a promise, forecast, guarantee, or advice**, and they are not MoonDAO's opinion of any outcome's likelihood.

**4.2 Non-custodial.** MoonDAO does not take custody of the funds you trade with. Collateral and Outcome Tokens are held by smart contracts and by your own wallet, secured by your own private keys. All bets, positions, resolutions, and redemptions are recorded on a public blockchain (currently Arbitrum One) and are publicly visible and permanent.

**4.3 Collateral and mechanics.** Bets are denominated in ETH, wrapped to WETH inside the contracts. When you place a bet through the DePrize bet router, approximately 5% of the ETH you send is paid to the Prize Pool as a prize contribution (Section 10) and approximately 95% is used to buy Outcome Tokens from the Market at the then-current price, less the Market fee (Section 11). While a Market is open you may sell Outcome Tokens back to it at the then-current price ("**cash out**").

**4.4 How Outcome Tokens pay.** For a Market with **N** outcomes:

| If the Market resolves… | An Outcome Token for that outcome redeems for… |
|---|---|
| To that outcome (it **wins**) | 1 unit of Collateral per token (its full share) |
| To a different outcome (it **loses**) | **Nothing — the token is worthless** |
| By **Equal-Payout Resolution** (Section 8) | **1/N** of a unit of Collateral per token |

**4.5 Not an investment.** Outcome Tokens are instruments for expressing a view on a specified event. They are not shares, debt, deposits, or investment products; they carry no ownership, dividend, interest, or governance right; and MoonDAO makes no representation that they will hold or gain value. **You may lose the entire amount you put into any position.**

**4.6 Third-party technology.** The Service relies on the CTF, an LMSR market-maker implementation, the Arbitrum network, wallet software, RPC providers, and other third-party technology that MoonDAO does not control. Section 22 of the Agreement (Third-Party Websites) applies.

**7.2 Resolution process.** When MoonDAO believes the Resolution Event may have occurred, or the deadline has passed, the following process applies unless the DePrize Rules provide otherwise: (a) the ERC reviews the available evidence and publishes a non-binding memorandum; (b) the Senate votes to declare a winner (a named Competitor or the Open Field) or to declare **No Winner**; (c) MoonDAO publishes the Senate result on the Site and opens a **challenge window of not less than seventy-two (72) hours** during which any person may submit evidence that the result is inconsistent with the DePrize Rules; (d) if a material challenge is received, the Senate may re-vote; and (e) the Oracle reports the final result to the CTF. The Senate's determination under the DePrize Rules is final and binding on all participants, subject only to Section 17.

**7.3 Finality and permissionless redemption.** The on-chain report of a result is **irreversible**. Once the Oracle has reported, the result cannot be changed, reversed, or refunded by MoonDAO, by you, or by anyone else, even if the report is later shown to be mistaken. After the report, redemption is permissionless: you (or anyone acting on your wallet's behalf) may redeem Outcome Tokens directly against the CTF or through MoonDAO's redemption helper. MoonDAO has no obligation to redeem for you or to remind you to redeem, and unredeemed positions remain claimable only for as long as the underlying contracts permit.

**7.4 The Market and the Prize are separate.** The Market resolves solely on the Resolution Event. Whether the winning Competitor (or Open Field entrant) is eligible to receive, claims, or is actually paid the Prize is governed by the Prize Rules and **does not affect your Outcome Tokens**. In particular: (a) if the winner is not a Prize-Eligible Winner (for example because it declines the Prize, cannot be verified, is a public body that cannot accept it, or is a sanctioned person), Outcome Tokens for that outcome still pay as winning tokens; and (b) the disposition of an unawarded Prize Pool is governed by the Prize Rules and creates no claim for bettors.

**7.5 Milestones do not delay bettors.** Bettor redemptions are available once the Oracle reports a winner. ## 8. Cancellation, No Winner, and Equal-Payout Resolution

**8.1 Refund-Terminal States.** A DePrize may be **Cancelled** by MoonDAO before a winner is reported (for example, because the capability becomes impossible, illegal, or moot, because of a material defect in the contracts or Rules, or because Applicable Law requires it), or may end with **No Winner Declared** because the deadline passed or the Senate so determined. Cancellation requires a public notice period of at least **seven (7) days**, recorded on-chain, during which new bets are closed and you may sell your position.

**8.2 Equal-Payout Resolution.** In any Refund-Terminal State the Oracle reports an equal payout for every outcome, so **every Outcome Token — whichever outcome you backed — redeems for 1/N of a unit of Collateral** (N = the number of outcomes in that Market). This is the standard mechanism used by non-custodial prediction markets to void a market.

**8.3 What this means for you — read carefully.**
- Equal-Payout Resolution is based on the **Outcome Tokens you hold**, not on the ETH you paid. **It is not a return of your stake.**
- The break-even price is 1/N. A position bought **above** 1/N (a favorite) **will redeem for less than it cost**; a position bought **below** 1/N (an underdog) may redeem for more. Example (N = 3, 1/N ≈ 33%): a token bought at 86% redeems for about 33% of its purchase price; a token bought at 3% redeems for roughly eleven times its purchase price.
- Only Outcome Tokens you **still hold** at resolution redeem. Positions you cashed out earlier are unaffected.
- The 5% prize contribution and Market fees are not returned by Equal-Payout Resolution (see Section 10 for the Project Token's separate mechanism).

**8.4 ETH only; no top-ups.** Redemptions are made in ETH (unwrapped from WETH) regardless of how you funded your bet. Gas is your responsibility. No fiat refunds are issued, and MoonDAO is not responsible for changes in the value of ETH between your bet and your redemption. You accept Equal-Payout Resolution as the **exclusive** remedy for a Refund-Terminal State. MoonDAO does not guarantee return of principal and has no obligation to top up, backstop, or supplement the on-chain payout.
