MoonDAO DAO LLC is the controller of personal data described in this Notice. Contact details are in Section 12.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

**2.1 Data you provide.**
- **Authentication data.** If you sign in through our authentication provider (Privy), the identifier you use — a wallet address, email address, phone number, or a Google, X/Twitter, Discord, or GitHub account identifier — and any embedded wallet address created for you.
- **Acceptance and eligibility records.** Your confirmation that you accept the Terms (including the version accepted), and your representations that you are not a U.S. Person, are not located in a Restricted Jurisdiction, are not a sanctioned person, and are not an Insider.
- **Communications.** Messages you send us (support, self-exclusion requests, misconduct reports, listing corrections), and any information they contain.
- **Prize claim data (competitors only).** If you claim a listing or a Prize under the Prize Rules: the legal name, registration details, and address of your organization; the names, roles, dates of birth, government identification, and contact details of authorized representatives and of beneficial owners of 25% or more; sanctions-screening results; IRS Forms W-9 or W-8 and taxpayer identification numbers; payout wallet addresses; and the contents of evidence submissions.

**2.2 Data we collect automatically.**
- **Blockchain data.** Your wallet address and every transaction it makes with the DePrize contracts — bets, cash-outs, redemptions, the amounts, the outcomes backed, and timestamps. This data is written to a public blockchain (currently Arbitrum One) by you, not by us, and is readable by anyone.
- **Network and location data.** Your IP address, the country/region/state derived from it (using request headers supplied by our hosting and CDN providers and, where needed, a third-party IP-geolocation service), device and browser type, operating system, language, referring URL, and the pages and actions you take on the Site.
- **Analytics and cookies.** With your consent where required, we use Google Analytics (via Google Tag with consent mode) and Vercel Analytics to understand how the Site is used. These tools use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies and collect pseudonymous identifiers, page views, events, and approximate location. A `cookie_consent` value is stored in your browser to remember your choice. An access-control cookie is set when you enter a password-gated area of the Site.
- **Wallet-screening data.** Results of screening your wallet address against sanctions and risk lists, where we perform such screening.

**2.3 Data from third parties.** Blockchain explorers and RPC providers (transaction data); IP-geolocation and VPN/proxy-detection services (location and connection type); sanctions, restricted-party, and blockchain-analytics providers (risk indicators); identity-verification providers (verification outcomes for competitors); public records and press (for competitor evidence review).

We do not collect precise geolocation, biometric data (other than any identity-document checks performed by a verification provider for competitors), or data about your health, religion, or political opinions.

## 4. Blockchain Data — Please Read

Blockchain transactions are **public, permanent, and outside MoonDAO's control**. When you bet, cash out, or redeem, your wallet address and the details of the transaction are recorded on Arbitrum One and can be viewed by anyone using a block explorer, forever. MoonDAO cannot edit, delete, or restrict access to this data, and rights of deletion or correction under privacy laws cannot be applied to it. If you sign in with an email or social account, MoonDAO can associate your wallet address with that account; third parties may be able to do the same using other information about you. Consider using a wallet dedicated to the Service if this concerns you.

## 5. Cookies and Analytics

- **Consent.** Where required by law, analytics tags fire only after you accept via the cookie banner. In jurisdictions that do not require prior consent, analytics may be enabled by default; you can change your choice at any time using the banner or your browser settings.
- **Google Analytics** collects pseudonymous usage data; Google's use of that data is described in Google's privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics browser add-on.
- **Vercel Analytics** collects aggregated, privacy-focused page-view and performance data.
- **Strictly necessary storage** — the access-control cookie and your cookie-consent preference — is used regardless of consent because the Site cannot function without it.

The Privacy Policy's statement that the Site does not use cookies or tracking technology does not apply to the Service; this Section 5 describes the technologies actually in use.

## 6. How We Share Personal Data

We share personal data only as described below.

- **Service providers** acting on our instructions: authentication (Privy); hosting, CDN, and edge networking (Vercel, Cloudflare); analytics (Google, Vercel); caching (Upstash); IP-geolocation and VPN detection providers; blockchain RPC and indexing providers (including thirdweb) and price-data providers (including Etherscan); sanctions, restricted-party, and blockchain-analytics providers; identity-verification and tax-form providers for competitors; email and support tooling.
- **MoonDAO governance participants.** Members of the Eligibility Review Committee and, where needed for a Senate determination, MoonDAO governance participants may receive competitor evidence submissions and claim information relevant to determining the Winner. Disclosures and recusals under the Prize Rules are published.
- **Public disclosure.** The Winner's legal name, the award, the Prize amount, and the payout address are published. Findings of misconduct under the Terms may be published.
- **Legal and compliance.** Regulators, law enforcement, courts, tax authorities, and our professional advisers where required or permitted by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
- **Corporate events.** A successor to the DePrize program, subject to this Notice.

Third parties you interact with independently — your wallet provider, the blockchain network, block explorers — process your data under their own policies.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to opt out of analytics, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise them, contact us under Section 12. We will verify your request (for example, by asking you to sign a message from the relevant wallet) and respond within the time required by applicable law. You may appeal a decision by replying to our response.

**Limits.** We cannot delete or alter blockchain data (Section 4). We may retain data we are legally required to keep, including acceptance and eligibility records and prize tax records, and data needed to enforce the Terms or defend claims.

**Analytics opt-out.** Use the cookie banner, your browser settings, or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

**Self-exclusion.** You may ask us to exclude your wallet from the Site (Terms, Section 11.5).

**U.S. state residents.** Although the Service is not offered to U.S. Persons, U.S. visitors to the Site may exercise the rights above. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information other than for the purposes permitted by applicable state law.

**EU, EEA, and UK.** The Service is not offered to persons in these jurisdictions, and we do not knowingly process their personal data. If you believe we hold your data in error, contact us and we will delete it where we are able to.
