## Earn rewards for your unique contributions!

Whether it's something outside the scope of projects, or an extension of them, your efforts won't go unnoticed.

**Current path:** become a [Citizen](https://moondao.com/join), then submit proof of work on the [Contributions](https://moondao.com/contributions) page. Senators review submissions for that quarter’s Contributor Circle. Rewards are allocated on [Projects](https://moondao.com/projects) with the rest of the quarterly cycle. See [Contribute](/docs/Onboarding/Contribute) and the [Project System](/docs/Projects/Project-System).

### What You Need to Know

#### How to Contribute to the Community Circle

1. **Join Discord** and introduce yourself if you have not already.
2. **Become a Citizen** at [moondao.com/join](https://moondao.com/join). Submitting on the website requires citizenship.
3. **Post your work** on the [Contributions](https://moondao.com/contributions) page (the form asks for proof of work and impact).
4. **Senators accept** eligible contributions into that quarter’s Contributor Circle.
5. **Get rewarded** at the end of the quarter in locked $vMOONEY plus the cycle’s cash-side asset (currently USD-pegged stablecoins).

#### Ways that you can contribute

- **Outreach and Partnerships:** Networking, partnership development, influencer outreach.
- **Community Engagement:** Event hosting, member onboarding, awareness campaigns.
- **Content Creation:** Blogs, social media posts, videos, memes, etc.
- **Technical Development:** Software, analytics, and fulfilling technical needs.
- **Research and Analysis:** Data analysis, market research, feasibility studies.
- **Other:** Check the [jobs board](https://moondao.com/jobs) for opportunities to engage with active projects, or if you have other ideas for how you can help support the DAO, let us know.

#### Pro Tips for Success

- **Network and Engage:** Presence and consistency builds trust, and goes a long way towards deeper integration into the community. Attending events like our weekly town hall gives you the contextual awareness to understand the DAO’s needs and values.
- **Demonstrate Impact:** Clearly present the results and impact of your work with specific and measurable metrics. Work without clear results will not be considered for rewards.
- **Be proactive:** Once you have a strong understanding of how you can contribute, simply start contributing! The most successful members at MoonDAO are active self-starters.

_Need Help? Reach out on Discord in our [support channel](https://discord.com/channels/914720248140279868/1178835616713154601) for any questions about this process._

### Legacy Discord / Coordinape flow

Earlier quarters used a Discord `community contribution` channel, a two-core-contributor vouch, and Coordinape slash commands (`/coordinape config`, `/coordinape contribution`). That path is retired. Do not use those commands as the official submission method.
