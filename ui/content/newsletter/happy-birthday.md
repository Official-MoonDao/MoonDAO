## Human Lunar Mission

Unless you've been sleeping under a rock, you've probably heard about the Artemis II mission which launched from Kennedy Space Center two days ago, marking the **first time humans are heading to the Moon in over 50 years.**

As of today, the Orion spacecraft has successfully completed its translunar injection burn and is on the way. The four person crew is executing a 10-day trajectory that features a lunar flyby before a planned Pacific Ocean splashdown around April 10.

More than anything, we LOVE how global attention has returned to the Moon and space industry as a whole. This is *just the beginning* of a very exciting time for lunar activity...
