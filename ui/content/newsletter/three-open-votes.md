## 🚨Q3 Retroactive Rewards

We currently have three active votes that need your input! Here’s a brief rundown of each proposal:

[MDP-155: Q3 2024 Incentives](https://vote.moondao.com/#/proposal/0xeb8080c307faf35ecab2910831de90aeddd0711aa5090af74f99214b96484f57) Decide on the distribution of rewards for four recently completed projects. Choose from one of the Rocketeer recommendations (R1-R7) or the average distribution. Each option reflects different percentage allocations based on evaluations and final reports. This is probably the biggest impact vote every quarter, allowing the community to give contributors feedback. 🚨**Voting is about to close for this one!**

[MDP-156: Eiman’s Journey: Inspiring the Next Generation through Space and Social Change](https://vote.moondao.com/#/proposal/0xcfcb0eb3b8a0f0d5ba7d64cc3e44dccca9604b2d1e0a401e1468c848134c0f3b) Support the creation of a five-part educational series documenting Dr. Eiman Jahangir’s journey to space, visiting Metro Nashville Public Schools to inspire students, especially from underrepresented communities, to pursue STEM careers.

## New Worlds Conference

MoonDAO will be boots on the ground during the New Worlds conference in Houston, Texas on November 1-2.

New Worlds 2024 is coming to Space Center Houston, and we have arranged an exclusive discount for the MoonDAO community. If you are interested in joining us, please reach out!

If you're interested in hanging out with Tim Dodd, Vanessa Wyche, Rod Roddenberry, and a bunch of amazing Astronauts, then this is a must-attend conference for you.

There’s a reason they call this conference the “SXSW of Space” … it’s the industry’s Alternative Space Event for those opening up the new frontier and those curious about the behind-the-scenes stuff going on in the industry.
