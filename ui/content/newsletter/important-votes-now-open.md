## Bite-Sized Space News

## Relativity Makes History

Relativity’s Good Luck, Have Fun mission lifted off from Florida last Wednesday shortly before midnight. Terran 1, the world’s first 3D-printed rocket, cleared the tower and passed Max Q, propelled toward the heavens by a beautiful blue flame. While the rocket successfully reached Max Q, main engine cut off, and stage separation, it fell short of orbit, with the second-stage engine failing to generate thrust after separation. The two-stage Terran 1 is the first rocket ever built primarily via 3D printing. This Terran 1 was about 85% 3D-printed by mass, and Relativity hopes to boost that to 95% on future vehicles.

Terran 1 was the first U.S. developed methane fueled rocket (which is why the flame had its unique color) to attempt an orbital launch, and could have been the first worldwide to reach orbit. Last year, Beijing-based company Landspace's Zhuque-2, another methalox rocket, was launched but likewise failed to reach orbit. While the ultimate goal was not reached, Relativity still [proclaimed the attempt to be a huge success](https://twitter.com/thetimellis/status/1640444713630326784) in terms of what they learned and pushed forward a lot of innovative new technologies in the launch industry.

## Private Views of the Moon

The ispace lunar lander, Hakuto-R Mission 1, recently shared a stunning view of the Moon captured after entering lunar orbit last week. There are multiple companies and countries pushing space exploration back to the Moon and MoonDAO plans to be part of these efforts.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/kKx6dJFaJJ5VkxTssFbZmZ)

Hakuto-R M1 is the inaugural mission of Tokyo-based ispace’s lunar exploration program which hopes to provide a low-cost delivery service to the Moon’s surface for private and public partners. The lander will attempt to reach the surface of the Moon in late-April (exact date still TBD), and, if successful, will be the first private mission to do so.\
The mission launched abroad a SpaceX Falcon 9 last December and took the slow and scenic route to the Moon, having just reached a stable lunar orbit after performing a lunar orbit insertion maneuver on March 21st.

## Town Hall

🎧 [Listen Here](https://twitter.com/OfficialMoonDAO/status/1640362193630769152)

Today’s MoonDAO Town Hall on Twitter covered some important updates, including the report from Blue Origin’s investigation of the anomaly on last year’s NS-23 flight that resulted in an emergency ejection of the capsule. The conclusion was that an overheated engine nozzle caused the New Shepard rocket failure last year. The capsule was uncrewed and the safety escape system worked as expected. [Read the Blue Origin findings here](https://www.blueorigin.com/news/ns-23-findings/).

Blue Origin expects to be flying again by the end of the year, starting with uncrewed missions before returning to crewed flights. All of this has implications for our second astronaut, Yan Kejun, who is scheduled to fly with Blue Origin in the future. Yan has his visa interview in about one week to be able to travel to the United States, and MoonDAO has done a lot of leg work and support to try and ensure that this visa application attempt is successful.

Beyond that, the town hall focused mostly on discussing the projects and proposals that are currently up for vote on Snapshot, so be sure to give it a listen to get more context about what each proposal will do and hopes to accomplish.

## Discord Restructuring

We completed a major revamp of the MoonDAO Discord to try and simplify and streamline the process and make it easier for everyone to find the most pertinent information. The biggest changes came by drastically reducing the guild-specific discussion forms which have been merged into one central discussion form, retiring guild voice channels and guild announcement channels, and the addition of a new generalized guild onboarding channel with tags.

We're also using [Guild.xyz](https://guild.xyz/moondao) now instead of Collab.land to verify the roles and permissions of our users. So, if you haven’t yet, you will need to connect your wallet in the 🔑│5-verify-roles channel on Discord or visit [https://guild.xyz/moondao](https://guild.xyz/moondao) to get started.

We will eventually be phasing out the old Collab.land roles, so please link your Discord and wallet to the new system soon and claim your cool new roles!
