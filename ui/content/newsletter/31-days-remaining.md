## Town Hall Recap: Frank White &amp; Astronaut Ron Rosano

This week's MoonDAO Town Hall featured a live conversation between Frank White and Virgin Galactic astronaut Ron Rosano, the 668th human to reach space. The two discussed what the Overview Effect means in practice, how commercial spaceflight is expanding access beyond governments and billionaires, and why missions like "Send Frank to Space" matter for shifting global consciousness.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/gSugq_o1XQY" title="video preview" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Ron shared his firsthand experience aboard Virgin Galactic's Mission 04 and reflected on how seeing Earth from above fundamentally changed his perspective. Frank described nearly four decades of studying astronauts' accounts of the Overview Effect and his hope that finally experiencing it himself will bring fresh insights back to the broader community.
