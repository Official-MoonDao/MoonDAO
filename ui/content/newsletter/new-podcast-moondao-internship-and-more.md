## Northern Lights Dazzle

Last weekend treated residents across the U.S. to a rare display of the northern lights, thanks to a powerful solar storm. The spectacle, typically seen in more northern latitudes, extended as far south as Texas and Florida.

This event was fueled by strong solar flares emitted by the sun since Wednesday, leading multiple coronal mass ejections (CMEs) into the Earth's outer atmosphere. The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration issued a Severe (G4) Geomagnetic Storm Watch, a rare occurrence, with Extreme (G5) conditions reaching Earth last Friday.

The heightened solar activity, due to the sun being at the peak of its 11-year cycle, provided optimal conditions for this widespread sighting.

## Starship IFT-4 in One Month?

Elon Musk provided an updated timeline for SpaceX's next Starship test flight, estimating it to be ["probably 3 to 5 weeks"](https://twitter.com/elonmusk/status/1789367327290712532) away, although they had initially been targeting May. The fourth launch aims to push the Starship beyond its previous limits, particularly in terms of maximum heating.

The company has been conducting static fire tests and preparing the vehicles for the mission, although logistical challenges, such as securing a launch license from the FAA, still need to happen before we get a launch date.

Ship 31 also appeared to experience some sort of anomaly during recent cryo testing, [as captured by NSF](https://twitter.com/NASASpaceflight/status/1789823633335943209), although details remain sparse.
