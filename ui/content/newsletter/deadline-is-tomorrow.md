## Rewards for Independent Work

We also have a pool of funds set aside to reward independent, mission-aligned work that has been completed during Q1. Yes, this could be anything that helped MoonDAO or advances our multi-planetary mission. This could be related to your participation in events, research, outreach, etc.

Here's how it works:

1. [Submit details](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdtHRzqDAAe1TOZ7Bp03TKVbxLFZzJeeKSUDQ-BpIZtDPxJWw/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=103874706116231728729) of what you did and its impact by April 15. The work should have already been completed during Q1, between Jan 1 - Mar 31 and not something planned for the future. Be sure to add proof of work with links or photos or documentation.
2. Our senators will review your work and distribute a percent of the reward pool to each individual based on the perceived impact of their work.
3. Once final calculations are made, we distribute ETH and vMOONEY voting power to individuals.

You've got until April 15th to submit your independent contributions for Q1, and until April 9th to submit your project proposal for Q2. **Let's go!**
