## Welcome to the Q4 cohort: 13 live projects

Congrats to the three newest projects approved for the Q4 cohort:

- [Spaceflight Training Scholarships](/project/276) (The Inspired 24): helping its spaceflight training courses offer lower tuition and full scholarships for underrepresented spacefarers.
- [Lunar Ledger API for Mission Control](/project/274) (Open Lunar Foundation): an open API so anyone can pull standardized data on lunar missions.
- [Analog Space Suit Upgrade](/project/273) (Austrian Space Forum): upgraded analog suits for simulated Moon and Mars missions.

That brings MoonDAO to 13 live projects. Tune in tomorrow, Thursday, Nov 5 at 3 PM ET, for Project Demo Day at Town Hall. Hear from all 13 teams about what they are building and how you can get involved, and bring your questions to the live chat.

## Executive Lead election closes Sunday

[Voting is open](/elections/executive-lead-third-seat-2026) for the third Executive Lead seat. Meet the candidates:

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/K9TEI-Dhky0" title="video preview" allowfullscreen></iframe>

- **Trent "Moonshot" Tresch:** over ten years in aerospace, from spacesuits to stratospheric balloons, and founding director of the University of Arizona’s Center for Human Space Exploration. His platform: make MoonDAO cash positive through a DAO-owned for-profit arm, technology development, and media and brand deals.
- **Anastasia Ad Astra:** a member since MoonDAO’s early days with an organizing and space background. Her platform: win grants and outside funding for hardware projects, set up joint research with universities, and grow Citizenship through career benefits and partner networks.

It is a ranked-choice ballot weighted by vMOONEY, and it closes in a few days. The same page also has a separate confirmation vote for Pablo Moncada-Larrotiz’s second term.
