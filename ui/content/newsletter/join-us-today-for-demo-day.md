## Proposal Updates

MoonDAO voters cast their votes on two big proposals recently. One was a proposed change to the Projects System which would have instituted seasonal governance, but was [voted down by a narrow margin](/proposal/193). This means that we'll be continuing with our standard proposal and projects system for Q4.

We have approximately $80,000 available in ETH in retroactive rewards for Q4, so we'd love to see your space project ideas become a reality. Submit them to [#ideation in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/914720248140279868/1027658256706961509), check out the [proposal process](/projects-overview), and put them up for vote to the MoonDAO community.

All MoonDAO projects are created by self-starters with a cool idea that they want to see become reality. Everything being done, is being done by someone like you.

<a href="https://discord.com/channels/914720248140279868/1027658256706961509" data-button="true">Share Your Idea</a>

The second vote was to [fund the Executive Branch operations](/proposal/194) for the next six months, which was approved by a wide margin. This will provide funding for the EB through the end of March 2026. Increasing revenue for the DAO through new initiatives and growing the number of Citizens will be a primary focus, along with normal ongoing operations, bug bashing, and general improvements, such as bringing our voting system in-house to not rely on external tools.
