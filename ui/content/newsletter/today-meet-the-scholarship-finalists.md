## **MoonDAO x WBA Scholarship Finalists Announced!**

We’re excited to share that the **10 finalists** for the **World’s Biggest Analog (WBA) Scholarship Awards** have been selected! These incredible candidates are preparing to take part in the largest space simulation of its kind, and **now it's up to YOU**, the MoonDAO community, to help decide how the $12,000 scholarship pool is awarded.

**Scholarship Awards:**\
🥇 1st Prize: $5,000\
🥈 2nd Prize: $2,500\
🥉 3rd Prize: $1,000\
🌍 All other finalists: $500 each
