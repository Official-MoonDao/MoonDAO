## Anniversary of the Columbia Shuttle Tragedy

February 1st marked the tragic 20th anniversary of the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster, which broke up while re-entering the atmosphere over the skies of Texas and Louisiana. An investigation determined that a hole was punctured in the leading edge of one of Columbia's wings when a piece of insulating foam from the external tank peeled off during launch and struck the wing. During re-entry, the hot gasses penetrated the interior of the wing and destroyed the support structure, causing the shuttle to break apart.

The seven crew members who died aboard this mission were: Rick Husband, Commander; William C. McCool, Pilot; Michael P. Anderson, Payload Commander; David M. Brown, Mission Specialist 1; Kalpana Chawla, Mission Specialist 2; Laurel Clark, Mission Specialist 3; and Ilan Ramon, Payload Specialist 1.

You can see a compiled and synchronized version of [how the events unfolded in this YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oNmR2YZO2gw).

The anniversary of this tragedy comes just days after the Challenger disaster which happened on January 28th, 1986. These dates are sobering reminders of the dangers of space travel and the continued efforts toward creating safe and rapidly reusable rockets.

## Treasury Transparency

The [Treasury Transparency report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mgka4_S9_UfS8wbx5Pgx9RoBxZv7d1Eq/edit) was recently released by MoonDAO contributor Luffy54, which aimed to analyze all the incoming and outgoing transactions in the MoonDAO treasury over the course of 2022. While MoonDAO's treasury transactions are *always* transparent, it can sometimes be difficult to make sense of it all and truly understand where each transaction is going and what the purpose is -- something this project meant to clarify.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/jzH4VRAFT9DYjikHwroFUP)

The report details how much has been spent during each phase of MoonDAO, includes an analysis of more recent projects and how much funding has been dedicated to each, the funds remaining in different project multi-sigs and so much more.

We highly encourage all MoonDAO members to take a look at the report to gain a better understanding of our finances and historical spending to have more context when considering future proposals, votes, and expenditures.

[Read the Treasury Transparency Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mgka4_S9_UfS8wbx5Pgx9RoBxZv7d1Eq/edit)
