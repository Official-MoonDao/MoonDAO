## NASA Artemis Changes

NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman has announced a sweeping overhaul of the Artemis program designed to accelerate mission cadence and address ongoing development delays. The agency is canceling the Exploration Upper Stage to standardize the Space Launch System and will pivot toward commercial alternatives. This restructuring significantly alters the near-term mission manifest.

Artemis III, scheduled for 2027, will no longer land on the Moon but will instead serve as a low-Earth orbit test flight to dock Orion with Starship and Blue Moon landers. The first crewed lunar landing is now targeted for Artemis IV in 2028 with a follow up mission happening in 2028 with Artemis V. This was framed as a strategic adjustment intended to mitigate risk and establish a sustainable schedule of annual lunar flights.
