### The Space Acceleration Network

We strategically focused on the scalable systems required to coordinate a global community and deploy capital with precision. We have evolved into a robust operational network powered by three interconnected pillars.

**A Global Talent Network:** We are focused on providing a seamless experience for every member of our network. Our community grew to 162 Citizens from over 30 countries this year, supported by:

- **The Onboarding Stack:** Frictionless social/email login with self-custodied wallets.
- **Financial Rails:** Integrated Coinbase on-ramps and a native MOONEY Uniswap portal, along with liquidity rewards.
- **Contribution Protocol:** A streamlined process to earn ETH and MOONEY for verified value creation.

**Specialized Teams:** We developed the infrastructure to onboard external organizations, from startups to non-profits, onto the platform. In 2025, we successfully onboarded 20 teams, ranging from non-profits, start-ups, and publicly traded companies, each operating with autonomous on-chain treasuries via Safe multi-sigs.

**Governance Stack:** We started migrating to a 100% in-house governance stack to ensure full protocol independence. This year, 23 proposals were successfully processed, supported by a total UI/UX overhaul and a new Dashboard for superior user experience.

### Capital Deployment

Part of MoonDAO's ongoing operations is to deploy direct community-governed capital toward the frontier. In 2025, we sent a total of **$184,000** to projects and contributors.

Over half of that was distributed by our retroactive rewards model which ensures accountability. By rewarding demonstrated impact rather than just promises, we align incentives across the entire ecosystem.

**Some 2025 Project Highlights:**

- **MOONEY to the Moon:** Successfully landed a physical wallet and bounty on the lunar surface early in the year.
- **Art Rocket Brazil:** An outreach project in collaboration with the Unity Foundation, sending artwork from cancer patients to space via a Soyuz rocket.
- **Lunar Open Source Rover Standards:** Establishing collaborative frameworks for the next generation of lunar hardware.
- **Rodent Research-20:** Granting vital support to ASRI and Kansas University for space biology research.
- **World's Biggest Analog Scholarships:** 10 scholarships to support participants in analog astronaut research at locations around the world.

### The MoonDAO Launchpad

The MoonDAO Launchpad is a platform for space organizations to raise funds from a global community while generating recurring revenue for the DAO treasury.

Our proof-of-concept, the **Inspiration4 Spacecamp Initiative**, validated the model:

- **Capital Raised:** Over $10,000 in 30 days.
- **Engagement:** 50+ individual contributors.
- **Industry Connection:** This initiative strengthened our ties with Space Camp and the Inspiration4 group.

With this technical stack de-risked, we are on track for a **bigger public launch of the MoonDAO Launchpad in Q1 2026.**
