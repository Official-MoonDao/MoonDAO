## ULA Vulcan Centaur

United Launch Alliance's (ULA) Vulcan Centaur rocket embarked on its inaugural flight, Cert-1, lifting off from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida at 2:18 a.m. EST.

The launch saw the rocket propelled by two solid rocket boosters and two Blue Origin-built BE-4 first-stage engines, carrying Astrobotic's Peregrine moon lander (more on that in a minute) hosting 20 different payloads, including five NASA science instruments.

By all accounts, this first launch of the Vulcan Centaur and the new BE-4 engines by Blue Origin all performed flawlessly.

Unfortunately, the lunar lander aboard may be in trouble...

## Astrobotic's Peregrine Lander

Pittsburgh-based Astrobotic Technology faced a critical setback after its lunar lander, Peregrine, encountered issues soon after launching aboard ULA's Vulcan Centaur towards the moon. After an improvised maneuver to reorient the solar panels toward the sun, the spacecraft has suffered “critical” propellant loss attempting to correct itself, likely jeopardizing the mission's primary goal of a soft moon landing on February 23.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/jU7FRFhCBDu7YWGh3oBLWw)

The Astrobotic team just receives the first image of the lander in space after fully charging the battery and will be "using existing power to perform as many payload and spacecraft operations as possible." This situation is still playing out in real time. [Follow Astrobotic on X](https://twitter.com/astrobotic) for periodic updates about the state of the lander and what other alternative mission profiles might be viable at this time.

Astrobotic's Peregrine is the first attempt from the United States to land on the Moon since 1972, but it isn't the only attempt this year. This is just the first of a series of commercial moon landing missions supported by NASA’s Commercial Lunar Payload Services program, or CLPS, to open the way for NASA’s crewed Artemis missions to the lunar surface. Houston-based Intuitive Machines is set to launch in mid-February.
