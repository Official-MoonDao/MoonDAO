Your vote here will help determine how to distribute up to 17.09 ETH ($65k) and 8.53M MOONEY (locked for four years as vMOONEY) to projects and contributors based on the voting results, minus any upfront payments. 10% of that total is reserved for community contributions.

**The process is simple:**

1. Connect your wallet
2. Distribute points to the project (totaling 100!)
3. Submit with wallet confirmation!
