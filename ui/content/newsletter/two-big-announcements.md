## Who’s Going to the Moon?

Earlier today, NASA announced the four astronauts that will be part of the Artemis II mission which will be the first humans to travel back to the Moon for the first time in more than 50 years! Without further ado, introducing the crew of Artemis II:

Commander - Reid Wiseman [@astro\_reid](https://twitter.com/astro_reid)\
Pilot - Victor Glover [@AstroVicGlover](https://twitter.com/AstroVicGlover)\
Mission Specialist - Christina Koch [@Astro\_Christina](https://twitter.com/Astro_Christina)\
Mission Specialist - Jeremy Hansen (CSA) [@Astro\_Jeremy](https://twitter.com/Astro_Jeremy)

These three Americans and one Canadian are scheduled to launch on a 10-day mission around the Moon no earlier than the end of 2024. Wiseman, Glover and Koch are all experienced astronauts, but this will be Hansen’s first spaceflight. Canada was the first country to join the United States as a partner in the Artemis program and has played an instrumental role as a partner in the U.S. human spaceflight program, most notably for their Canadarm contribution to the space shuttle and to the ISS. Another robotic arm, the Canadarm3, will be built for the lunar-orbiting Gateway station.

Artemis II is targeting a launch in November 2024, and as a test flight, it will not go into orbit around the Moon but will fly past on a free-return trajectory that will bring them back home, even if the Orion engines do not perform as expected. Last November, NASA launched the Artemis I mission which was a test of the new Space Launch System (SLS) rocket and the uncrewed Orion capsule. After numerous launch delays, the first mission was declared a major success and the Orion spacecraft performed better than expected, despite losing more material from its heat shield during reentry than predicted.

Artemis II is the first crewed flight test on the agency’s path to establishing a long-term scientific and human presence on the lunar surface and will pave the way for the Artemis III mission later this decade. Artemis III would also mark the first time humans have touched down on the Moon since the Apollo program ended in 1972. The new age of lunar exploration is about to begin and we are so happy to have you along for that journey as part of the MoonDAO community.\
