## Upcoming Moon Mission

MoonDAO, in collaboration with LifeShip, is thrilled to be aboard the Firefly Blue Ghost lunar lander set to launch from Kennedy Space Center this week and land in the Mare Crisium basin approximately 40 days later. While MoonDAO members' DNA will not be aboard this flight, as originally envisioned, we are still part of a golden Lunar Pyramid filled with plant seeds, global artwork and other artifacts from humanity which will serve as a time capsule and monument, including a digital copy of the MoonDAO Constitution and the private key to an Ethereum wallet.

Within that wallet will be an onchain lunar treasure chest containing tokens and NFTs from digital communities such as Mr. Miggles, Own the Doge, Mumu the Bull, Pudgy Penguins, DOMS, and ElonRWA.

This lunar wallet awaits future explorers who might recover the Pyramid and return it to Earth in order to unlock 90% of the bounty while setting aside 10% to help fund a future space mission and bigger monument on the Moon. This challenge pushes humanity to innovate, sparking a new type of race to the Moon while cementing memes as part of our multiplanetary legacy.

This is one small step for MoonDAO, one giant leap for memekind—uniting humor, culture, and exploration for years to come.
