## PSYCHE!

NASA’s PSYCHE mission is a journey to a unique metal-rich asteroid orbiting the Sun between Mars and Jupiter. The spacecraft was launched on Friday the 13th (spooky!), aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket from Kennedy Space Center in Florida, heading to the metallic asteroid Psyche in the Main Asteroid Belt. The mission aims to study the asteroid Psyche, which appears to be the exposed nickel-iron core of an early planet, one of the building blocks of our solar system.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/5wYt923R49MeEgL7QE8vPW)

The mission aims to shed light on planet formation by studying the asteroid. The initial phase involves testing the solar electric propulsion system, and the spacecraft will pass Mars in May 2026 to reach Psyche by August 2029. In orbit, Psyche will employ various scientific instruments to examine the asteroid's composition, with data transmitted back to Earth using cutting-edge optical communications technology. The mission represents a significant step in understanding the universe's mysteries and planet formation.

## Annular Eclipse

The annular solar eclipse that occurred on October 14, 2023, was a rare celestial event that was visible across most of the Americas. During the eclipse, the moon passed between the Earth and the sun, creating a “ring of fire” effect in the sky. Millions of people across North, Central, and South America were able to witness this phenomenon.

The eclipse began Saturday morning in the United States and lasted for approximately 3 hours. The path of annularity, where the “ring of fire” effect was visible, spanned across eight U.S. states from northwest to southeast, between Oregon and Texas. The rest of North America and Central America, as well as most of South America, experienced a partial solar eclipse of varying obscuration.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/9TF8BSohBdCRSs6vZDhSfW)

The event was [livestreamed by NASA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LlY79zjud-Q) for those who were unable to witness it in person. Yours truly was on the ground in the path of annularity in Central Texas, and it was truly a remarkable sight to behold!
