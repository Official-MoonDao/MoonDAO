## Funding Space Event

We are also excited to invite you to **'Funding Space'** this Wednesday, January 28th. We will be streaming live on LinkedIn for a deep dive into the evolving space financing landscape.

[![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/8tQZdfoA8ZoALHXVNspzYf)](https://www.linkedin.com/events/7417226867855859712/)

**Date:** Wednesday, January 28th

**Time:** 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM UTC

**Link:** [https://www.linkedin.com/events/7417226867855859712/](https://www.linkedin.com/events/7417226867855859712/)

In this two-hour virtual event, industry pioneers, leading venture capitalists, and innovative founders will explore the intersection of deep tech and capital. We host this event on a significant date for the space community as we honor the legacy of the Space Shuttle Challenger astronauts and their January 28th, 1986 flight.

**OPENING KEYNOTE**

- **4:00 – 4:30 PM: 'Funding Space: Past, Present, and Future'** With **Frank White**, Author of *The Overview Effect*

**GUEST PRESENTERS**

- **4:30 – 4:50 PM:** 'Funding Space R&amp;D and Exploration Using Onchain Native Rails' with **Pablo Moncada-Larrotiz**, Founder &amp; Executive Lead at **MoonDAO**
- **4:50 – 5:10 PM:** 'The End of Moonshots: Why Space &amp; Deep Tech Are Entering Their Infrastructure Era' with **Alexandra Vidyuk**, CEO and General Partner at **Beyond Earth Ventures**
- **5:10 – 5:30 PM:** 'Funding the Final Frontier: How Capital Is Evolving to Support Space &amp; Deep Tech Infrastructure' with **Ruben Portela**, Founder of **Wise3 Ventures**
- **5:30 – 5:50 PM:** 'Two Cultures, One Stack: Building and Fundraising Between Space and Web3' with **Filip Rezabek**, Co-Founder of **SpaceComputer**
- **5:50 – 6:00 PM:** Closing thoughts with **Pablo** from **MoonDAO**
