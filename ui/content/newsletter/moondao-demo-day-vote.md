## Axiom-4 Returns to Earth

Axiom‑4, the fourth private mission to the International Space Station organized by Axiom Space and flown with SpaceX, splashed down safely off the California coast on 15 July 2025. Former NASA astronaut Peggy Whitson, now Director of Human Spaceflight at Axiom, completed her fifth flight and extended her United States record to 695 total days in space.

She flew with astronauts from India, Poland, and Hungary, marking the first official ISS missions for those countries. During their 18‑day stay, the crew conducted over sixty microgravity experiments and returned with valuable scientific samples.
