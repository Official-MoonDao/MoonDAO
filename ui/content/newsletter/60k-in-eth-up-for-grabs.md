## Bite-sized Space News

[**Crew‑11 mission launch**](https://www.spacex.com/launches/crew11)**:** A multinational crew (U.S., Japan, Russia) successfully launched on August 1st to the ISS aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 and Dragon capsule after an initial scrub. NASA will continue relying on Dragon for crewed flights through 2025 following Boeing Starliner delays.

[**U.S. announces lunar nuclear reactor plans**](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/08/04/nasa-china-space-station-duffy-directives-00492172)**:** NASA’s acting administration has issued a directive to fast-track development and deployment of a 100‑kW nuclear reactor on the Moon by 2030, to support sustained lunar presence and counter strategic moves by China and Russia.

[**Blue Origin launches NS-34 crewed mission**](https://www.blueorigin.com/news/new-shepard-ns-34-mission)**:** The suborbital flight carried six civilians, including TRON founder Justin Sun, past the Kármán line and back in Blue Origin’s 14th human spaceflight on August 3rd.

[**Congressional earmark to move a Space Shuttle to Houston**](https://arstechnica.com/space/2025/08/houston-youve-got-a-space-shuttle-only-nasa-wont-say-which-one/)**:** A provision in the “One Big Beautiful Bill” allocates funds to relocate a flown orbiter, likely Discovery, sparking legal disputes with the Smithsonian and concerns over total costs exceeding $300M.
