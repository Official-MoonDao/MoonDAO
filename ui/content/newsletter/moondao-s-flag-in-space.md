**MoonDAO's flag has officially flown in space for the very first time**—albeit a miniature version! Eiman carried a small, pocket-sized MoonDAO flag aboard the NS-26 launch and brought it out at apogee.

We’ve been gradually releasing highlights from Eiman's incredible journey as MoonDAO’s second astronaut.

The day after his flight, we gathered in El Paso for his first post-flight interview, capturing his thoughts and emotions as his lifelong dream of space travel came true.

In this insightful, half-hour conversation with our founder, Pablo Moncada, Eiman shares what it was like, how his life has changed since learning he'd be going to space, and what he envisions next.
