## Bite-Sized Space News

## NASA Crew 6 Launch

NASA’s Crew 6 was all set to take off from historic LC-39A aboard a Falcon 9 rocket en route to the International Space Station but the launch was scrubbed with less than two and a half minutes on the clock. NASA and SpaceX scrubbed Monday’s launch attempt to the ISS due to a ground systems issue.

Mission teams decided to stand down to investigate an issue preventing data from confirming a full load of the ignition source for the Falcon 9 first stage Merlin engines, triethylaluminum triethylboron (or TEA-TEB). NASA and SpaceX will forgo a launch opportunity on Tuesday, Feb. 28, due to unfavorable weather forecast conditions, and the next available launch attempt is at 12:34 a.m. EST Thursday, March 2, pending resolution of the technical issue preventing Monday’s launch. This will be the first crewed launch of 2023.

Safety always comes first though, especially for any crewed mission!

The Dragon capsule will carry an international mix of astronauts heading to the ISS, with two American astronauts, one Russian cosmonaut, and an astronaut from the United Arab Emirates on that country’s first long duration space mission. The crew consists of the two NASA astronauts, Stephen Bowen and Warren Hoburg, the cosmonaut, Andrey Fedyaev, and Sultan Al Neyadi from the UAE. Their mission is scheduled to last sixth months and will replace Crew 5 who will return to earth shortly after hand off.

2023 may prove to be a particular busy year for crewed Dragon spaceflights, with the upcoming launch of the Axiom-2 private flight to the ISS this May, and Jared Isaacman’s [Polaris Dawn](https://polarisprogram.com/dawn/) mission this summer which will try to reach the highest orbit ever flown, along with a Crew 7 mission later this year. We also have the upcoming crewed demo flight of Boeing's Starliner CFT-1 to the ISS to look forward to, so there should be an additional vehicle servicing the ISS as it completes its final years of service.

## Presidential DNA to Space

[Celestis](https://www.celestis.com/), the Texas-based space burial company, has announced that it will honor four former U.S. presidents - George Washington, Dwight D. Eisenhower, John F. Kennedy, and Ronald Reagan - by adding their symbolic remains to the upcoming deep space remembrance Enterprise Flight.

This flight will also include the remains of several deceased "Star Trek" legends and science fiction superstars, including Gene Roddenberry and James "Scotty" Doohan. The launch is expected to take place sometime this year inside a United Launch Alliance Vulcan rocket, which will carry special capsules containing the cremated remains and DNA samples of these historical and cultural icons.

MoonDAO is excited to be partnering with [LifeShip](https://lifeship.com/) to send the DNA of members from the MoonDAO community to space along with some of our foundational documents. This will be MoonDAO’s first small step on the lunar surface, and yes, you too can be part of a space mission and add your DNA to a time capsule of life from Earth launching to the Moon. As space exploration and access continues to open up, this will be MoonDAO’s first lunar mission, so you're not going to want to miss your chance to be part of this historic mission for the MoonDAO community.
