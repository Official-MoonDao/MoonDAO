The window for our first-ever **Seasonal Governance Cycle** is now open, and we're looking for builders.

As we shift from weekly ad-hoc voting to a strategic quarterly rhythm, we have allocated a dedicated budget to fund the most high-impact ideas in our ecosystem.

[![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/8rPYHuKNRAzp8sPWm1ei6w)](/propose)

**Submission deadline is January 15th. Maximum budget is 2.08 ETH ($6.6k) per project. Apply here:** [**moondao.com/propose**](/propose)**.**

<a href="/propose" data-button="true">Submit Your Proposal</a>

We will accept the top 50% of submitted projects, budget allowing. If you want to build with MoonDAO in early 2026, **you must submit now.** Once this window closes on January 15, the door for new funded projects will not reopen until Q2 in April.

**How to Apply:**

1. **Refine your Concept:** Pressure test your idea, get feedback, and find your team in the **#Ideation** channel on Discord. [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/moondao)
2. **Submit Your Proposal:** Formalize your plan and budget request in our governance portal. [Submit Here](/proposals)
