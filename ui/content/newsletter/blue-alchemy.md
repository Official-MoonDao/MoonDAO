## Bite-Sized Space News

## SpaceX Mass to Orbit

According to a recent [quarterly briefing by Bryce Technology](https://brycetech.com/reports/report-documents/Bryce_Briefing_2022_Q4.pdf), SpaceX nearly matched the world's tonnage launched into orbit in Q4 of 2022. During that period, SpaceX launched a total of 142,300 kg into orbit, surpassing China's space program in second place which stood at 69,900 kg, followed by NASA, Roscosmos, and Arianespace.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/fQo8EHxktsBSNuJDqH37SQ)

In response, [Elon Musk tweeted](https://twitter.com/elonmusk/status/1625536488422404097?) that the company intends to nearly triple its mass to orbit this year. Musk shared that SpaceX plans to launch around 400 tons of useful mass to orbit per quarter in 2023, although that may depend on whether Starship can get up and running before the end of the year – we are all still watching Boca Chica for more developments.

## Blue Origin Announcements

Blue Origin, the space technology company founded by Jeff Bezos, has demonstrated that it can [make solar cells out of simulated moon dust](https://www.blueorigin.com/news/blue-alchemist-powers-our-lunar-future) using a process called "molten regolith electrolysis". The company's approach, called "Blue Alchemist", generates all the necessary ingredients for a working solar panel and can scale indefinitely, eliminating power as a constraint anywhere on the moon.

The process involves extracting the key ingredients from the lunar soil by melting it and passing a current through it, producing oxygen as a byproduct, which can be used for astronaut life support or to help power rockets. Blue Origin's approach also produces silicon with 99.99 percent purity and found a way to use the byproducts of the process to create glass covers to protect the solar cells from the harsh lunar environment.

Blue Origin has also won its first [NASA award for its New Glenn rocket](https://www.nasa.gov/press-release/nasa-selects-blue-origin-to-launch-mars-magnetosphere-study-mission/), with the agency selecting the rocket to launch a pair of Martian smallsats. The rocket will launch the ESCAPADE (Escape and Plasma Acceleration and Dynamics Explorers) mission to Mars in late 2024, with the spacecraft entering orbit around Mars 11 months later.

## Town Hall about Good Governance

🎧 [Listen Here](https://twitter.com/OfficialMoonDAO/status/1627699677671092224)

Today’s town hall was a little different as we dived into a free-ranging discussion about good governance and incentives in light of the recent Snapshot votes with some clear intent to manipulate the outcome. For background, MoonDAO uses retroactive compensation where voters decide how much of a reward to dedicate to each project. The idea is that projects that produce outsized results will be given bigger rewards and projects that fall short in their outcomes will be given less.

This system can be prone to manipulation if someone is trying to inflate their numbers or get an outsized share of the pie for themselves and their project, so today’s discussion focused a great deal on how we can address these problems, how to increase voter turnout, and other ways to make our systems fairer and less vulnerable to manipulation.

If you’re interested in DAOs, good governance, and how MoonDAO can improve, we think you’ll enjoy [today’s conversation](https://twitter.com/OfficialMoonDAO/status/1627699677671092224). We’d love to hear your thoughts on some of these sticky issues that DAOs are trying to tackle.
