## Bite-Sized Space News

## Crew 5 Return

With the recent docking of Crew-6 at the International Space Station, NASA astronauts Nicole Mann and Josh Cassada, JAXA (Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency) astronaut Koichi Wakata, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Anna Kikina wrapped up 157 days in space and splashed down safely in the SpaceX Dragon Endurance off the coast of Tampa, Florida, at 9:02 p.m. EST on March 11th. The splashdown took place nearly 19 hours after the spacecraft undocked from the station.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/iNjiTsyxbdzU484Nxvtn6X)

During their mission, the Crew-5 team conducted various research projects to advance scientific knowledge. They explored hydroponic and aeroponic techniques for growing plants without soil, while also releasing Uganda and Zimbabwe’s inaugural satellites. The team studied the behavior of liquids in containers under simulated lunar gravity to improve the design of Moon rovers. In addition, they tested an on-demand system that can extract key nutrients from yogurt, kefir, and a yeast-based beverage. The crew members grew dwarf tomatoes to address the need for a continuous supply of fresh food in space and reinstalled the station’s bioprinting facility, which is part of a long-term plan to manufacture whole human organs in space.\
Speaking of the ISS, SpaceX is getting ready to launch Commercial Resupply Service mission 27 which is set to launch from Florida tomorrow evening -- see the upcoming launches section for more details.

## NASA Budget Proposal

The White House has requested a 7% increase in NASA's budget for next year, which amounts to $27.2 billion, with a focus on the Artemis program and funding for the deorbit of the ISS. The proposal fully funds the Artemis II and III missions, allocating $8.1 billion to the lunar exploration program, representing a $500 million increase over the previously enacted level.\
Additionally, the proposal includes funding for NASA to develop a space tug for the safe deorbiting of the ISS by 2030, which is the first time that funding has been allocated for the deorbit and decomissioning of this long-term space habitat. The budget also includes $949 million for the Mars sample return mission to bring Martian soil back to Earth, which could help put David Bowie’s question to rest and help scientists determine if there was ever life on the Red Planet.

It’s important to remember that the final allocation will likely differ as it is subject to approval from a divided Congress.

## Emergency Treasury Action

On Friday evening, MoonDAO held an emergency Senate meeting to discuss stablecoin risk in our treasury. For those unaware, the DAI stablecoin was depegging from its 1 USD rate due to fears about wider underlying assets and risk of contagion from the Silicon Valley Bank collapse.

The outcome of the meeting was that we held an emergency vote in the Senate to transfer our DAI reserves (approximately 600,000 USD) into ETH. The vote passed with 6 in favor and 1 against.

You can see the current status of our treasury here: [https://etherscan.io/address/0xce4a1e86a5c47cd677338f53da22a91d85cab2c9](https://etherscan.io/address/0xce4a1e86a5c47cd677338f53da22a91d85cab2c9)

This unprecedented move was done out of an abundance of caution with what the Senate felt was the best course of action to ensure the integrity of the treasury. The FDIC later announced that deposits at SVB will be fully backed, alleviating fears about stablecoins held at SVB, which allowed USDC and DAI to return back to 1 USD, after having dipped to as low as 90 cents on Friday night when the emergency vote was held.

## Q4 Incentives Distributed

Last Wednesday, DAI and Mooney payments were issued retroactively for projects completed during Q4 of 2022. If you were a contributor during that time period, your payments should have already arrived. Remember that DAI is sent as a lump sum payment while Mooney is streamed over the course of one year, as long as the member remains an active contributor to a project (Moon Settler or above).

These retroactive payments are meant to incentivize projects to go above and beyond and be rewarded by community vote for their work with a percentage of our treasury’s liquid tokens and provide a larger stake in MoonDAO governance via the geometric release of Mooney tokens.

We are quickly approaching another round of quarterly incentives at the end of the month which marks the end of Q1.
