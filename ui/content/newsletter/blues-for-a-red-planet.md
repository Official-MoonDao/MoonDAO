## Bite-Sized Space News

**Mars Rover:** The Perseverance Rover on Mars has stashed its [10th and final soil sample](https://twitter.com/NASAJPL/status/1620110707877617666) on the surface of the Red Planet which will act as a backup in case it is unable to deliver other onboard samples to a future Earth-bound rocket. The car-sized rover has been collecting dozens of samples during its search for ancient Mars life which could be returned as early as 2033 in a joint NASA-European Space Agency (ESA) if all goes according to plan. The Perseverance Rover landed in February 2021 near Jezero Crater along with its helicopter companion, Ingenuity.

Speaking of Martian Rovers, if you haven’t yet, be sure to check out the [Good Night Oppy](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/good_night_oppy) documentary about the story of the Opportunity Rover, it is an incredible story and a beautiful film.

**Virginia is for Launch Lovers:** Electron successfully [launched their first Electron booster from US soil](https://www.rocketlabusa.com/updates/rocket-lab-successfully-launches-first-electron-mission-from-u-s-soil/) in a mission called *Virginia is for Launch Lovers* (a play on the state’s motto) which deployed three satellites for their customer Hawkeye 360. The booster launched from NASA’s Wallops Flight Facility and soared over Virginia’s Eastern Shore shortly after sunset. This inaugural US flight paves the way for potential monthly flights from Wallops by Electron. The company is working on a larger reusable rocket called Neutron which would also launch from Virginia, although the first flight is expected no earlier than 2024.

## Town Hall

During today’s town hall, we covered a few important governance updates but we were also joined by Lennart Lopin and James Burk to discuss MarsCoin. [MarsCoin](https://www.marscoin.org/) launched back at the beginning of 2014 as a fork of LiteCoin and was dedicated to supporting the settlement of Mars and other space exploration technologies.

The conversation was wide-ranging, covering the background and tokenomics of MarsCoin, testing on-chain voting and governance, prototypes rolling out at the Mars Desert Research Station’s habitat in Utah, the pros and cons of an Ethereum token (such as that used by MoonDAO) vs a separate cryptocurrency with mining (such as MarsCoin), and so much more! There's a lot that we at MoonDAO can learn from an organization that has been involved in this space so long, so it was a privilege to get to chat with these two leaders in more detail.

The conversation with MarsCoin begins at about the half-hour mark of this two-hour town hall, be sure to [listen to the full conversation here](https://twitter.com/OfficialMoonDAO/status/1620089444459220992).

## Project System Updates

We are still working through some potential updates and changes to the MoonDAO projects system and are looking for input and comments in the Discord governance channels. For a little bit of context, we have generally operated by having small teams move forward on individual projects to allow those teams to operate somewhat autonomously. Sometimes it can be inhibiting or unhelpful to have the entire DAO or outside members with low context giving their opinions about projects in progress or having to weigh in about every small detail, thus the creation of teams.

MoonDAO uses a form of retroactive compensation for projects that have been completed and allows the token holders to allocate rewards to teams based on the benefit or impact of a project which is the origin of the incentives structure and retroactive compensation that we use with Coordinape.

MoonDAO is tweaking this process as we go and refining it as we learn more which is where Project V4 comes in. We are also exploring the idea of allowing people to permissionlessly move forward with projects if they don’t require upfront funding but still be eligible for retroactive compensation.

We are trying to shift focus toward projects that can generate revenue and sustain MoonDAO for the long term. Part of V4 is considering how we can do that in a way that incentivizes the people creating revenue for MoonDAO to be able to share in some of that revenue between the project team and the DAO to better align incentives. We welcome your comments and ideas for Project V4 in Discord.
