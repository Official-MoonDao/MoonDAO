## Mission Update: 71% Funded

We’re now **71% of the way** toward our goal to sponsor training hardware at Space Camp USA in Huntsville, Alabama, with **35 amazing backers** and just **9 days left** to reach the finish line!

[![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/8Wbpxw2NvfwnMW5whruCkn)](/mission)

A huge thank you to our astronaut **Dr. Eiman Jahangir**, who personally matched **$500 USD** in contributions last week, doubling the impact of our community’s support. Eiman’s generosity and belief in this mission embody exactly what MoonDAO is all about: working together across borders to empower the next generation of explorers.

[![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/4rBMUN8aKBPZBh5vRMfANC)](/mission)

This is an **all-or-nothing mission,** we must hit the minimum goal for the mission to launch and be funded successfully. Every single contribution counts, whether it’s $5 or $50, and your support today can help push us over the line.

Let’s finish strong and show what a decentralized, global community can accomplish together.
