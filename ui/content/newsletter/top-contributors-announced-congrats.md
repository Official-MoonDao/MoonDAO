## MoonDAO x LOGIC

MoonDAO cofounder Pablo Moncada-Larrotiz recently presented in front of the Lunar Operating Guidelines for Infrastructure Consortium (LOGIC), a DARPA-led group administered by JHU‑APL that’s shaping standards (like power, communications, navigation) for future lunar infrastructure, about blockchain infrastructure designed to support mission planning and interoperability on the Moon.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/XaDadnwc8-4" title="video preview" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Developed in partnership with ICON3DTech, this system enables trustless, peer-to-peer collaboration, crucial for environments like the lunar surface where connectivity is sparse and autonomy is essential.

The framework leverages a powerful stack of open-source technologies, including Ethereum, Arbitrum, IPFS, Tableland, Safe, and more, bringing credibly neutral solutions to off-planet coordination.
