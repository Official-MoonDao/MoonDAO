## Bite-Sized Space News

## SpaceX Starship Launch Scrub

Thousands of space enthusiasts from all over the world descended on Boca Chica, Texas, and tens of thousands more tuned in online for the first integrated flight test of the fully-stacked Starship rocket. The space industry was absolutely abuzz with excitement, but with just under nine minutes to go in the countdown, SpaceX announced an issue with the pressurization system on the first stage, the Super Heavy booster, and the launch attempt was scrubbed.

The attempt was then transitioned into a wet dress rehearsal in order to work through the rocket’s pre-flight procedures and use the opportunity to learn more about its systems. SpaceX will now wait for at least 48 hours before attempting another launch. As of this moment, a new launch time and date has not yet been announced, but signs seem to indicate that 4/20 is on the table.

Starship will be the most powerful rocket in history when it finally does take flight.

## Lunar Lander

Japan’s ispace announced that their Hakuto-R lunar lander will attempt its autonomous landing next week on April 25th. At this point, the lander has spent nearly one month orbiting the Moon and recently achieved the 100-kilometer circular orbit that is necessary to begin the lunar descent.

We’ve been following the progress of this important mission for months as it will represent the first lunar landing by a private entity – the first of many planned for the near future, including MoonDAO’s upcoming collaborative mission with Lifeship to send the DNA of members like you to the lunar surface.

You are made of star stuff, and now you have the opportunity to send a little piece of you back out into the cosmos while also being a pioneer in humanity’s return to the Moon.
