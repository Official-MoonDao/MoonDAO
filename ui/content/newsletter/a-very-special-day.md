## Zero Gravity (Part 1)

Last week in Houston, the MoonDAO team and [Space for a Better World](https://www.spaceforabetterworld.com/) had the privilege of launching Part 1 of our highly anticipated Zero-G experience. Although the actual zero-gravity flight segment was delayed due to unforeseen circumstances with ZeroG, we all know that scrubs are par for the course in the world of space exploration. Despite this temporary setback, we still had the privilege of spending an unforgettable day in the company of three iconic NASA astronauts: Nicole Stott, Doug Hurley, and the legendary Apollo Moonwalker, Charlie Duke.

## Starship Wet Dress Rehearsal

SpaceX's latest Starship prototype successfully [completed a "wet dress rehearsal"](https://twitter.com/SpaceX/status/1716979479845953646) at its Starbase facility in South Texas, loading over 10 million pounds of propellant into the Starship and Super Heavy in preparation for a second test flight.

<figure>

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/pB1MssMMiGb5L9L3inwjDE)

The company awaits regulatory approval from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (FWS) and the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) for the upcoming launch, following a previous mishap investigation that led to safety and environmental concerns. Despite the regulatory challenges, SpaceX has implemented required corrective actions and aims to resume test flights soon, emphasizing the importance of both safety and a rapid development and testing process for their prototypes.

Rumors from Starbase watchers are pointing toward November 6th as a tentative launch date for the second orbital flight test, although that is always subject to change. When it does happen, we’d love to get a small MoonDAO group together on the ground to watch another historic launch attempt of the world’s biggest and most powerful rocket!

## Blue Origin Lander

Blue Origin has revealed a full-sized mockup of its uncrewed Blue Moon lunar lander, designed to transport three tons of cargo to the Moon's surface as a part of NASA's Artemis program. The company plans to conduct a "Pathfinder Mission" to test critical systems, including the BE-7 engine, cryogenic fluid power, avionics, and precision landing. While future Mark 1 landers are intended for customer payloads, launch dates for these missions remain undisclosed.

This development coincided with a visit by NASA Administrator Bill Nelson to see the Blue Moon mockup and explore its role in maintaining a continuous human presence on the Moon.

In parallel, SpaceX continues to focus on the development of the Starship lunar lander for the Artemis program, with an emphasis on guidance, navigation, control, thermal control, and power generation technologies. However, concerns have been raised about the overall timeline for the Artemis 3 mission, emphasizing the importance of the success of the second orbital flight test of Starship/Super Heavy (OFT 2) and the need for a comprehensive focus on the entire Artemis mission enterprise.
