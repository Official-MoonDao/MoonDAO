## Bite-Sized Space News

## India Tests Crew Module

The Indian Space Research Organization (ISRO) successfully conducted a crew escape system test, bringing them closer to their goals of crewed spaceflight. The test involved launching a Gaganyaan crew module to demonstrate its safe separation from the rocket and its ability to protect future crews in the event of an ascent issue.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/8YFs6H2ceFLbTK5CSxXy62)

The test confirmed the system's performance as intended, with the crew module separating at 17 km, deploying parachutes for a safe descent. This achievement marks a significant step towards India's human spaceflight program, Gaganyaan, which is part of a broader space exploration plan outlined by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, including establishing an Indian Space Station by 2035, a Moon mission by 2040, and missions to Venus and Mars.

## NASA Moon Habs

NASA has partnered with ICON, a Texas-based construction technology company, to work towards its goal of establishing lunar habitats by 2040. ICON received funding from NASA in 2020 and an additional $60 million in 2022 to develop a space-based construction system that can 3D print structures on the moon, including rocket landing pads and habitats, using on-site concrete.

While 3D printing is considered a cost-efficient solution for housing, construction in space presents unique challenges due to the lack of readily available materials and the need for lightweight solutions. NASA and its partners are also exploring the development of space furniture and interior design, while scientists work on creating lunar concrete for construction. The moon is seen as a practical layover point for missions to Mars, where lunar resources can be used to produce rocket fuel.
