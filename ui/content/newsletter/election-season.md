This August MoonDAO will hold an election for our second leader of the executive branch.

Here's what you need to know:

### **Candidate Announcement Deadline**

If you are interested in running as a candidate for the position, you have until **Thursday, July 25th** to announce that you're running. Please post in the [🗳│election channel](https://discord.com/channels/914720248140279868/1263654497339572295) that you are running to officially submit yourself in the race. After we have all the candidates announced on the 25th, we'll have a one week period for candidates to state their platform and vision for MoonDAO, and we'll host a debate at the townhall on August 1st.

From there, we'll run a five day vote between the candidates to select the winner.

**In summary, here are the dates to look out for:**

- **Thursday, July 25th:** Deadline to announce your candidacy in the #election channel.
- **Thursday, August 1st:** Debate between candidates during our townhall.
- **Monday, August 5th:** Vote closes to select our next executive branch leader.

## Curious Discovery on Mars

NASA's Curiosity Mars rover made a significant discovery when it drove over a small rock in Gale Crater, revealing yellow sulfur crystals inside. This finding marks the first time such crystals have been observed on Mars and is notable because the rock is composed of pure elemental sulfur, unlike the previously detected sulfur-based minerals.

This discovery surprised scientists, as sulfur forms under specific conditions not previously associated with the region Curiosity is exploring. Further investigation revealed an entire field of similar sulfur rocks, indicating a unique geological environment. Curiosity's continued exploration and drilling, particularly into a large sulfur rock named "Mammoth Lakes," aims to provide deeper insights into the region's geological history and the broader history of Mars.

## New Space Documentaries

Two compelling space documentaries were released last week, offering unique insights into the rapidly evolving space industry. "Wild Wild Space," now available on HBO, tells the story of Astra, Rocket Lab, and Planet, featuring key figures who have played pivotal roles in these companies. Based on Ashlee Vance's book "When the Heavens Went on Sale," the documentary includes appearances by several individuals who have collaborated with or interacted with MoonDAO. Filmmaker Torsten Hoffmann spent three years investigating this emerging industry, which also features MoonDAO member and friend Ben Haldeman from [LifeShip](/lifeship). Later this year, MoonDAO will by flying with LifeShip aboard Firefly's Blue Ghost lander to the surface of the Moon for our very first Moon mission, including a digital copy of our constitution and a private key. Watch the Fortitude trailer [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=slrJOYoy4GU).

Both documentaries provide captivating perspectives on the frontier of space exploration and commercialization.

## Astra Goes Private

Speaking of Astra, which was prominently featured in Wild Wild Space, they have concluded their tenure as a public company by completing a privatization deal with its co-founders, Chris Kemp and Adam London, for 50 cents per share, after its shares had plummeted by over 99% since its peak following its 2021 public debut via a SPAC merger. The company, once valued in the billions, now stands at a market capitalization of about $12 million. Financial difficulties and operational setbacks, including the poor performance of its Rocket 3 vehicle, led to consideration of bankruptcy before accepting the privatization offer. Future plans remain vague, but Kemp has hinted at a renewed focus on developing Rocket 4 and hopes for more successful launches as a private entity.
