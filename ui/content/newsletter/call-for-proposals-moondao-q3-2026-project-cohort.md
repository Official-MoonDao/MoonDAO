MoonDAO utilizes a dual funding mechanism designed to prioritize execution and accountability:

- **Seed Capital:** Approved proposals receive up to $4,682 USDC in funding.
- **Retroactive Rewards:** Upon completion, projects become eligible for additional capital from our quarterly rewards pool based on impact, as decided by voters.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/kT5xid8GDWM9eQBkM2X98b)

Here is a cross section of active projects currently executing with MoonDAO capital:

- **Deployable Video Payloads:** Engineering a compact, deployable stabilizing camera capsule to capture third person telemetry and visual data of sounding rocket deployments.
- **Maldives Space Research Balloons:** Conducted an intensive workshop for the Maldives Space Research Organization to build, fly, and recover high altitude stratospheric balloons capable of carrying heavy payloads to near space.
- **ACMA Ignites:** Funding all-women student teams in Colombia to compete in the national rocketry contest, providing technical training and hardware for the next generation of aerospace engineers.
- **Moon Founder's Club:** Organizing a series of technical meetups and a dedicated MoonDAO track at the NASA Space Apps Challenge in Florianópolis, Brazil, to onboard regional builders into lunar settlement development.
