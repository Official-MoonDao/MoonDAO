Here’s a little more background about Nicole Stott to help you think of some awesome questions…

Nicole Stott is an American engineer, artist, and a retired NASA astronaut. She served as a flight engineer on ISS Expedition 20 and Expedition 21 and was a mission specialist on STS-128 and STS-133 and she spent 104 days in space. Nicole and her Expedition 21 crewmate Jeff Williams participated in the first NASA Tweetup from the station which also involved the first live Twitter connection for the astronauts. She is one of just over 500 people to enter Earth’s orbit, the 52nd woman to do so — and the 10th woman to perform a spacewalk. After 27 years of working at NASA, she retired in mid-2015.

Besides being an astronaut, Nicole is also known for her artistic work. She combines her art and spaceflight experience to inspire creative thinking about solutions to our planetary challenges, raise awareness of the interplay between science and art, and promote the amazing work being done in space to improve life on Earth.
