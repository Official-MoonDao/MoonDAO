## Bite-Sized Space News

Blue Origin's New Shepard sub-orbital rocket is set to start launching again! This is great news as we select our second astronaut via the [Ticket to Space Sweepstakes](https://app.moondao.com/sweepstakes). The return to flight will be an uncrewed science mission and was initially scheduled for Monday, December 18th, but experienced a scrubbed launch due to a ground system issue. The launch will be attempted again today (Tuesday, December 19th) with a launch window opening at 10:37 AM CST / 16:37 UTC and will be livestreamed.

The NS-24 mission will mark the rocket's return to flight after an anomaly in 2022, where an engine nozzle problem led to a launch abort system activation, safely landing the capsule during an uncrewed scientific flight. Blue Origin made design changes to address the nozzle issue and implemented corrective actions outlined by the FAA.

The company is also hard at work on the New Glenn rocket, which founder Jeff Bezos has said that we'll see launch in 2024. If you haven't already, be sure to check out [Lex Fridman's recent interview with Jeff Bezos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcWqzZ3I2cY), with a deep dive into Blue Origin's mission, his experience with the Overview Effect on a New Shepard flight, updates on New Glenn, and much more during this two-hour interview.
