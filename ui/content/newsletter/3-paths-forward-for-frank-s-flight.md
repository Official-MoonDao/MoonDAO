## Send Frank to Space Mission UPDATE

This week, we hosted a pivotal MoonDAO Town Hall focused on the **“Send Frank to Space”** campaign.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/lj7X2ODjbKc" title="video preview" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Having successfully raised over $170,000 for the mission, our team has been busy over the past 30 days, actively engaged in negotiations with nearly a dozen suborbital and stratospheric carriers across three continents.

The primary takeaway from these discussions is that no stratospheric balloon company is commercially operational yet; every provider is still navigating hardware development and testing under genuine uncertainty. While a suborbital contract with Virgin Galactic is on the table, we currently only have enough capital to cover the initial deposit, meaning a suborbital flight would require extended fundraising.

To determine the best course of action, the community is being asked to [read the report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V9ZzZCXrRu7ZjTI9rwE41W_FD8q__OnfUGOgzJr--B4/edit?usp=sharing), watch the town hall, and weigh in on three distinct paths forward:

- Option A: commit immediately to a single stratospheric seat for Frank and defer the candidate seat,
- Option B: keep options open, place refundable deposits, and continue fundraising for two seats, or
- Option C: activate full pro-rata refunds for all contributors.

No binding vote is happening yet; instead, members are encouraged to join the MoonDAO Discord to share feedback on these paths (or others) and the team's evaluation framework before a formal, token-weighted vote is launched.
