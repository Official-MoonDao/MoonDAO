## Vote: Constitutional Amendments

For constitutional amendments we have a two week time window for token voting. However, due to a change in our voting platform (Snapshot) we were not able to update the time window for the latest proposals.

To overcome this we've re-submitted **MDP-179 and MDP-180** a second time for another five days so that people have two weeks since it was originally submitted to vote on the proposal, we will aggregate the result between the two voting periods to determine whether the proposal passes.
