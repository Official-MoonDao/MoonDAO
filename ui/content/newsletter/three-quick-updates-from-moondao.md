## \2) Doug Hurley on MoonDAO Live

We've got NASA &amp; SpaceX astronaut Doug Hurley 🤯 on MoonDAO Live this Friday (1/19) at 12pm EST. Let us know any questions you'd love to ask, give the [X post a like and share, and set a reminder](https://twitter.com/OfficialMoonDAO/status/1746955479564337354) to join!

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/eG6FmsZqBdoYKv8qSjLRvn)

Doug piloted Space Shuttle missions STS-127 &amp; STS-135, the final shuttle flight. He flew to space for the 3rd time as commander of Crew Dragon Demo-2. Demo-2 was the first crewed spaceflight from American soil since STS-135 and Doug became, together with Bob Behnken , the first astronaut in history launching aboard a commercial orbital spacecraft.
