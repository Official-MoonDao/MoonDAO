## MoonDAO Votes

First, there are two new project proposals recently posted to Snapshot. Be sure to get your vote in by this weekend to get your opinion heard. Here is what we are currently considering:

[**MDP-84 MoonDAO Donation for Gitcoin Grants Budget Extension:**](https://snapshot.org/#/tomoondao.eth/proposal/0x4e63d1a558fb4b7f80aff5c13cba70b361db26b5d3ef1e446e0e7d4c40d2de59) The Gitcoin Grants round team is asking for a budget extension of 1,000 DAI and 200,000 MOONEY to distribute as bounties to support the Gitcoin Grants Round. Unused funds for the bounties will be returned to the treasury.

[**MDP-85 MoonDAO Digital Asset Marketplace:**](https://snapshot.org/#/tomoondao.eth/proposal/0x4301f6d50502fc96f9469b222c135560024cb8dac8e51f70af7d16d4558e1d8e) The MoonDAO Digital Asset Marketplace empowers vMooney holders to list their digital assets for sale in exchange for Mooney. The marketplace opens up opportunities for anyone holding Mooney to explore and purchase unique listings or place competitive bids.

Please refer to our [Stake to Vote Guide for a video walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suKBRdo2T6I) or reach out on Discord if you need help to start voting.

## Call for Project Proposals

We are excited to announce the launch of the Decentralized Science round, a new initiative aimed at supporting and funding innovative projects in the field of space science and beyond.

As a researcher, scientist, or innovator, this is your opportunity to showcase your work and receive the funding and support you need to take it to the next level. As a supporter of MoonDAO and Web3, we encourage you to help spread the word about this great opportunity ([Retweet this](https://twitter.com/OfficialMoonDAO/status/1648353831603011585)).

We invite all those with a passion for space exploration and research to submit their project proposals for consideration. The Decentralized Science round seeks to support innovative projects that can make a real difference in the world of public goods, whether in areas such as climate change, healthcare, education, or beyond. Here are a few steps you'll need to take:

1. Create your [Gitcoin Passport here](https://passport.gitcoin.co/).
2. Create your project on [Gitcoin Builder here](https://builder.gitcoin.co/).
3. Explore the [Main and the Featured rounds here](https://grants.gitcoin.co/#rounds).

To submit your proposal for the DeSci round, [simply follow this link](https://explorer.gitcoin.co/#/round/1/0x6e8dc2e623204d61b0e59e668702654ae336c9f7).

You can participate in one Main round and many Featured Rounds. If you are a founder with a focus on Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) there is also a Bonus Round. Be sure to [check out the Grantee portal for additional information](https://gitcoin.notion.site/Gitcoin-Grantee-Portal-6adfc92627474bd48a5dfcd1e8438d20).

This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the advancement of decentralized science and push the boundaries of what we know about the universe. Don't miss out on the chance to get the funding and support you need to make your vision a reality.

Please note *this week is the last week* to submit your proposal, so don't hesitate to act. We look forward to receiving your ideas and supporting the next generation of space science innovators.

**The submission deadline for the Main Rounds is April 21st. For the DeSci round we have a deadline on April 24th.**
