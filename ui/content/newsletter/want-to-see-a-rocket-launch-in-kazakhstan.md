## Open Vote

Voting is open for proposed changes to the Projects System (V8).

If approved, this proposal would:

- Add a deadline for project submissions for the third week of the quarter, establishing a seasonal governance cycle rather than rolling governance.
- MoonDAO will fund the top 50% of projects that are submitted that quarter and pass senate review, via weighted vMOONEY vote, or the top 4 proposals, whichever is more.
- Project budgets get subtracted from the retroactive rewards, and the rest is distributed via retroactives.
- Among other changes.

Please see the full proposal and Pull Request here: [https://www.moondao.com/proposal/193](/proposal/193)

## Space Mice Approved!

Last week, MoonDAO voters have voted in favor of MDP-191: ASRI Rodent Research-20 “Save the Space Mice”. This proposal will fund the Rodent Research-20’s mice colony for a period of three months (90 days) in order to buy the mice time while broader fundraising campaign preparation efforts are completed to find a long-term solution for the offspring of these space flown mice.
