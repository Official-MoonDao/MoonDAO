## Q1 Call for Projects

**Looking to fund your space initiative in Q1 2026?** We're about to kick off the new Seasonal Governance Cycle for projects with our first cohort. The window is getting short, and the standards are higher.

We are concentrating capital on high-impact work where only the top 50% of ranked proposals will be funded.

![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/oNXanhVJwV9sJeDRL6ck2v)

Here is the timeline:

**January 6:** Q4 deadline for all retroactive work and Q4 final reports. If you built it in 2025, submit it to get rewarded by voters. Project Leads should be sure to submit their final reports by then, and Citizens please [submit any independent contributions](/contribute) from Q4 '25.

**January 15:** The deadline for NEW Q1 project proposals. If you want funding between Jan–Mar, you must submit by this date, or wait until the Q2 cohort in April. This new cycle includes a Senate review and workshop week (Jan 15–22) to refine your pitch before the community vote on Jan 22.

Get the conversation started in [#Ideation](https://discord.com/channels/914720248140279868/1027658256706961509) and refine your ideas over the holidays.

You can learn more about the new seasonal governance process in [last week's Town Hall presentation](https://www.youtube.com/live/Tn7Egt9mPNs?si=DjPquAB18P_INa7Y&t=276) by Ryan. Last week's town hall also featured an amazing presentation by Trent and William about their experience at the Soyuz-28 launch from Baikonur, Kazakhstan, the culmination of the Art Rocket Project with the Unity Foundation in Brazil.
