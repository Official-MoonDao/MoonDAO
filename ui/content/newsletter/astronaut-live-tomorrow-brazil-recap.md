## Chris Sembroski of Inspiration4

Join us tomorrow for a very special town hall with Chris "Hanks" Sembroski, commercial astronaut with the Inspiration4 mission.

We’ll explore his path to space, his experiences on orbit, and what it means to carry that mission of inspiration forward.

[![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/d9RUpX9h6ugYB9QjqPC36W)](https://luma.com/6ze59uzd)

Inspiration4 launched four years ago and was the first orbital spaceflight with only private citizens aboard. It was also part of a charitable effort on behalf of St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital in Memphis, Tennessee. The mission launched the SpaceX Crew Dragon Resilience with Chris, Jared Isaacman, Sian Proctor (a MoonDAO citizen), and Hayley Arceneaux on a three-day orbital mission.

## MoonDAO-Unity Foundation Brazil Trip

What just unfolded in Brazil was nothing short of extraordinary. With our second astronaut, Dr. Eiman Jahangir, and Russian cosmonaut Denis Matveev, we traveled through four cities alongside the Unity Foundation, bringing the spirit of space exploration to life. From visiting seven hospitals and homes for children with cancer, to speaking with doctors, students, and professors at various universities and schools, to connecting with the Brazilian Space Agency, everywhere we went, we witnessed the power of space to inspire and unite.

> [Check out this reel from Itamed Hospital](https://www.instagram.com/p/DOrRWw3jihH/)

[![](https://embed.filekitcdn.com/e/5MgHd6kkhZ8fZBAgpuZgWA/2WfjCHTDyJp8KzKXyZywXa)](https://www.instagram.com/official_moondao)

We also ventured to Habitat Marte, one of the Latin America's premier analog stations, gathered the local space community in Rio de Janeiro for a beachside celebration under the full moon (with a surprise visit from the rain), and started a space inspired art rocket mural with a local street artist in Foz do Iguacu. The energy, hospitality, and warmth we felt from Brazil and the Brazilian people was overwhelming, and we are deeply grateful for the welcome we received at every step.

Updates from this journey have been flowing on [our Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/official_moondao), with much more still to come. This trip was profoundly impactful, and it’s hopefully only the beginning of what’s to come.
