§ 03 — Governance

Members decide. The chain remembers.

Everything is proposed by a member. There is no product team deciding what gets built. A proposal is written, debated in the open, and put to a vote of token holders.

Lock $MOONEY to become a voter and co-govern the treasury. Voting weight comes from how long you commit, not how loudly you post.

Two people have flown on this system. Coby Cotton was selected as MoonDAO's first astronaut by onchain vote. Dr. Eiman Jahangir was selected as the second by open sweepstakes.

Both selections, and the funds behind them, are auditable line by line. That is the whole point.
