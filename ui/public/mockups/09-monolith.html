01 — ProgramsSix active

Everything below has already happened.

These are not roadmap items. Each was proposed by a member, approved by a vote of the membership, and executed against a published budget.

01

Human spaceflight

The first crowdraised astronaut was selected by onchain vote and flown in 2022. A second everyday person was selected by open sweepstakes and flown in 2024.

02

Research funding

More than $600,000 allocated across seventy-five research and engineering projects through open community governance.

03

Astronaut training

Zero-gravity flights and analog missions that prepare the next generation of travelers before there is anywhere to go.

04

The Space Acceleration Network

Connects individuals and organizations with the funding, tools, and operational support to turn a proposal into hardware.

05

A constitution on the Moon

A charter for self-governance, written by members and delivered to the lunar surface in early 2025.

06

An open treasury

Every proposal, vote, and payment is published and independently verifiable. There is no discretionary spending.
