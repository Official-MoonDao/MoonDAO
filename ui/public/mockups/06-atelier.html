The hard part was never the rocket.

Getting to the Moon has always been an organizational problem as much as an engineering one. Somebody has to decide it is worth doing, find the money, and keep going for longer than an election cycle.

MoonDAO is an attempt to do that without asking anyone's permission. Twelve thousand people pooled eight million dollars, and then decided together — in public, by vote — where it should go.

What follows is not a pitch. It is the record.

Governance

Weight comes from commitment.

Lock $MOONEY to become a voter and help direct the treasury. Your influence scales with how long you are willing to stay, not with how loudly you argue. There is no application and no gate.

Two people have already flown on decisions made this way — Coby Cotton by onchain vote in 2022, and Dr. Eiman Jahangir by open sweepstakes in 2024.
