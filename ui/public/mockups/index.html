MoonDAO · Design exploration · 2026

Ten directions for a lighter, quieter MoonDAO.

Every direction below is a full homepage built from the same content — the same numbers, the same programs, the same names — so the only thing that changes between them is the aesthetic argument. All of them are light, mostly white, and deliberately restrained. Open any one, scroll the whole page, and tell me which are directionally right.

01 · Cupertino

Inter Tight

The most literal reading of the brief. Apple's exact grammar: translucent nav, enormous centered headline, alternating white and pale-gray bands, soft-shadowed cards, one deep blue for links. Product photography on black does the emotional work so the type never has to raise its voice.

Feels likeapple.com

02 · Swiss Technical

IBM Plex

An engineering document rather than a marketing page. The 12-column grid is left visible as hairlines, sections are numbered like clauses, and metadata is set in monospace. Programs are rows in a spec table, not cards. Reads as competent and unexcitable.

Feels likeA published spec

03 · Editorial

Newsreader

A serious publication. Centered serif masthead, rules above and below the nav, a light display serif with true italics, two-column body copy with a drop cap, and a centered pull quote. Argues that MoonDAO is an institution with something to say, not a product with something to sell.

Feels likeA quarterly journal

04 · Gallery

Cormorant Garamond

The quietest of the ten. Type is small and widely letterspaced, images are enormous, and the page is mostly air. Every block of text is a wall label. The confidence comes entirely from how much is left out — closest to a museum, furthest from a website.

Feels likeA gallery catalogue

05 · Blueprint

Geist · JetBrains Mono

Ink on drafting paper. A faint grid runs the full page, corner registration marks frame the sheet, a dimension line measures the distance to the Moon, and a drawn orbital diagram carries the history instead of a photograph. Technical without costume.

06 · Atelier

Fraunces

Warm rather than clinical. Bone paper instead of white, a soft optical serif, desaturated imagery, and a clay accent. Centered compositions and long line-height give it the pace of a luxury house — expensive taste, spoken quietly.

07 · Rational

Archivo

Vignelli-school modernism. Heavy black rules, a strict grid, uppercase headlines set very tight, and figures treated as graphic elements. The most assertive of the ten — included deliberately, so you can tell me where the ceiling on volume actually is.

08 · Softlight

DM Sans

Modern light-mode software. A floating pill nav, pale aurora wash behind the hero, rounded cards, and gentle shadows. The friendliest and most approachable option, and the one closest to current product-site convention — which is either an advantage or the whole problem.

09 · Monolith

Geist

Typography as the entire hero. No image above the fold — just an enormous, very tightly tracked statement that fills the viewport, then a single pale full-bleed photograph. Everything after is rules, numerals, and space. Confident and slightly severe.

10 · Institutional

EB Garamond · Public Sans

A report to members. Engraved seal, ruled tables of programs and chronology, numbered sections, superscript footnotes, and a single navy. Makes MoonDAO read like a standing institution that publishes its accounts — the most credible, and the least fashionable.

Feels likeA national laboratory
