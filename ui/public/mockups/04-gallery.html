Index of works

Six programs, each one begun by a member and settled by a vote.

I · Spaceflight

Human spaceflight

The first crowdraised astronaut, chosen by onchain vote. A second everyday person, chosen by open sweepstakes. Both flown.

II · Research

Space R&D funding

More than $600,000 allocated to seventy-five projects through open community governance.

III · Training

Astronaut training

Zero-gravity flights and analog missions for people who intend to go.

IV · Network

Space Acceleration Network

Funding, tools, and operational support for individuals and organizations building in space.

V · Charter

A constitution on the Moon

Written by members, delivered to the lunar surface in early 2025.

VI · Treasury

An open treasury

Every proposal, vote, and payment published and independently verifiable.

On method

Nothing here was decided behind a door. A member writes a proposal, the membership argues about it in the open, and the result is settled on a ledger nobody can quietly revise.
